Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your dental concerns

We recommend visiting the dentist at least twice a year for routine checkups and professional cleaning. However, patients with gum disease or other conditions may need more frequent visits. During your appointment, we'll create a personalized schedule based on your oral health needs.

Yes, professional teeth whitening at Dentiva is completely safe when performed by our qualified dentists. We use high-quality, clinically tested whitening agents that are safe for your enamel. Results typically last 6-12 months depending on your lifestyle and habits.

The implant procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won't feel pain during the treatment. You may experience mild discomfort or swelling after the procedure, which is normal and can be managed with prescribed pain medication. Most patients report minimal discomfort during recovery.

We accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment methods. We also offer flexible payment plans and installment options for major treatments. Please discuss payment arrangements with our reception team during your consultation.
